Affiliate program management

What a TikTok Shop affiliate agency actually does

A TikTok Shop affiliate agency recruits creators to promote your products through TikTok Shop’s affiliate program, manages samples and commission offers, briefs the content, and tracks which creators drive sales. Adeptecom does all of that for you, using a creator network we have already vetted.

Who it’s for

US and UK ecommerce and DTC brands that sell on TikTok Shop and want creator-driven sales without building an in-house affiliate team — new shops that need their first creators posting, and established shops whose affiliate program has stalled.

How we run your affiliate program

  1. Product & commission strategy — we pick the SKUs to push, choose between open and targeted collaborations, and set commission rates and hybrid deals around your margins.
  2. Creator matching — we shortlist creators from our 10,000+ vetted US & UK network by niche, audience, and price point, instead of mass cold outreach.
  3. Sample seeding — samples go out with structured follow-up, because most of the value is lost when nobody chases the post.
  4. Content briefs — every creator gets the one angle and hook that sells your product on camera.
  5. Tracking & scaling — we rank creators by the sales they drive, re-invest in winners, and hand the best content to paid as Spark Ads.

What it costs

Creator and affiliate program management is included from our Growth plan at $999/month, billed monthly with no long-term lock-in. Scale ($1,499/month) adds advanced creator scaling across the US and UK. Creator commissions are a percentage of each sale, settled through TikTok Shop, and are separate from our fee; any flat-fee creator deals are agreed with you upfront.
