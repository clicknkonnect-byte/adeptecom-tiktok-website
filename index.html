Common questions

TikTok Shop questions, answered

What does Adeptecom do on TikTok Shop?
We run TikTok Shop end to end and largely on autopilot. We connect your seller account through TikTok’s official APIs, our AI agents handle audits, affiliate and content research, competitor studies, and market-gap findings, and our US & UK creator network drives the sales, alongside Spark Ads, LIVE selling, and ongoing reporting.
Do you work with US and UK brands?
Yes. Our strongest results are in the US and UK, and we have hand-built a network of high-converting creators in both markets across skincare, household, footwear, supplements, and baby categories, where we know the compliance and creator landscape.
Can you help if my listings keep getting rejected?
Yes, this is one of the most common problems we solve. Rejections usually come down to missing documentation such as an SDS, a wrong category leaf, or attribute and claim issues. We clear the account and get listings live.
Do you manage the affiliate and creator program too?
Yes. We source creators, seed samples, and run outreach at scale with hybrid and commission offers designed to get creators posting and reposting, all tracked in a founder-facing dashboard.
How fast can you set up a TikTok Shop?
Once the required documents and details are provided, setup typically takes a few business days. Category approvals can add time, and we manage that process for you.
Do you run ads and LIVE selling, or just setup?
Both. Setup is only the start. We run the affiliate program, Spark Ads built on proven creator content, and LIVE selling, and we scale on real return across the business.
