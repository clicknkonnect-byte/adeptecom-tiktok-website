How much does a TikTok Shop agency cost?

A straight answer on 2026 US pricing — retainer vs. commission vs. hybrid models, what actually drives the price up or down, and how to compare quotes.

TikTok Shop GMV in the US grew sharply through 2026, and a wave of agencies now sell "TikTok Shop management" at wildly different price points. Before you sign anything, it helps to know what the market actually charges, what structures exist, and what each one is supposed to cover.

1. The short answer

Published US TikTok Shop agency pricing commonly runs from a few thousand dollars a month for a narrow scope up to $15,000+ a month for full-service management, according to industry reports, often with a percentage of ad spend or GMV layered on top. Adeptecom's plans start at $499/mo (Launch), $999/mo (Growth), and $1499/mo (Scale) — billed monthly, no long-term lock-in.

2. What does "TikTok agency account price" actually mean?

"Account price" usually means one of two different things, and it's worth separating them before you go quote-shopping. Most people asking this mean the monthly fee to have an agency manage the TikTok Shop seller account they already have — listings, creators, ads, LIVE, or some mix of those. A much smaller group means something riskier: a price to buy or sell an existing TikTok Shop account outright.

For the management question, the answer is the same range covered above: a few hundred dollars a month for a lean, single-workstream scope up to $15,000+ a month for full-service agencies running every channel at scale, per published industry pricing. Adeptecom's three flat plans — Launch ($499/mo), Growth ($999/mo), and Scale ($1499/mo) — sit at the lower end of that range because we connect directly to your TikTok Shop seller account through TikTok's official Seller APIs rather than staffing a large account-management team.

Buying or selling a TikTok Shop seller account is a separate, materially riskier practice that can violate TikTok's seller terms and put your catalog, payouts, and verification status at risk — Adeptecom doesn't broker or recommend it. If what you actually need is help running the account you already have, or getting a new one set up correctly, that's standard TikTok Shop setup services and ongoing management, priced the way the rest of this page describes.

3. The three common pricing models

Most TikTok Shop agencies price on a flat monthly retainer, a percentage of GMV or ad spend, or a hybrid of the two — and the model matters as much as the number.

A flat retainer is predictable and keeps the agency focused on efficient growth rather than raw topline. A percentage-of-GMV or ad-spend model (commonly cited in the 10–20% range for GMV share, or a management fee on ad spend) scales with your sales — which sounds aligned, but can quietly outgrow your margin once volume increases. A hybrid charges a lower base retainer plus a smaller performance cut. Ask any agency to walk through the exact formula with a real revenue number before you compare quotes side by side.

4. What actually drives the price up or down

Scope is the single biggest cost driver — a shop-setup-only engagement is priced very differently from one that also runs creators, ads, and LIVE.

The workstreams that typically get priced separately (or bundled only in higher tiers) are: shop setup and compliance, listing optimization, creator/affiliate program management, Spark Ads and GMV Max management, and LIVE selling production. Ad spend itself sits outside the agency fee entirely — it's paid to TikTok, not the agency — so always separate "what we pay the agency" from "what we spend on media."

5. What a typical retainer does — and doesn't — include

Very few retainers cover every workstream well, so the scope you're buying matters more than the headline price.

Before comparing two quotes, confirm in writing: how many listings are optimized per month, whether creator outreach and sample fulfillment is active or passive (Open Collaboration only vs. targeted invites), whether Spark Ads and GMV Max are actively managed or just "available," and whether LIVE selling is planned and hosted or left entirely to you. See how we scope this across Setup & API Onboarding and Listing Optimization.

6. Hidden costs beyond the agency fee

The agency retainer is rarely the full cost of running a TikTok Shop — creator commissions, product samples, and ad spend sit on top of it.

Budget separately for: creator/affiliate commissions (paid per sale, on top of any base fee), product samples and shipping for creator seeding, Spark Ads and GMV Max media spend, and platform and payment processing fees. A credible agency will show you this as two numbers — the management fee and the expected spend — not one blended figure. 8. Questions to ask before you sign

The fastest way to compare quotes fairly is to ask every agency the same five questions.

What exact workstreams are included at this price? Is there a percentage fee on GMV or ad spend, and what's the formula? Who pays for creator commissions and samples — you or the agency? Is LIVE selling included or a paid add-on? And what's the minimum term, if any? Get the answers in writing before you compare numbers.
