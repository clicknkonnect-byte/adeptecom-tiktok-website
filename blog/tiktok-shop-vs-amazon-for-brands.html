TikTok Shop vs. Amazon for brands

Both channels sell to US shoppers, but they do different jobs. Here's how TikTok Shop and Amazon actually compare on reach, cost, conversion, and brand-building — and how to decide where to put your next dollar.

Brands rarely need to pick one of these channels forever. They need to know which one to prioritize right now, and why. TikTok Shop and Amazon reward different skills and different kinds of products, so the right call depends on your category, your content capacity, and how fast you need revenue versus brand equity.

1. What's actually different between the two

Amazon is search commerce: shoppers arrive already looking for a product. TikTok Shop is discovery commerce: a creator's video introduces the product before the shopper has gone looking for it.

That single difference drives everything else on this page. Amazon rewards a well-optimized listing that converts intent that already exists. TikTok Shop rewards ongoing creator content that creates intent where there wasn't any a minute earlier — see our Listing Optimisation approach for the Amazon-style side of that and the US & UK Creator Engine for the TikTok side.

2. Which platform has more reach?

Amazon is still the far larger US marketplace by total sales volume. TikTok Shop is a fraction of that size but is growing much faster year over year, according to public industry reporting on both channels.

Multiple 2026 industry reports put TikTok Shop's US GMV growth in the double- or triple-digit range year over year, even as its absolute volume remains well behind Amazon's established marketplace. For a new or smaller brand, that gap means Amazon offers more existing search volume to capture today, while TikTok Shop offers a faster-growing audience to build a position in before it gets more competitive.

There's also a meaningful audience overlap: public reporting on TikTok Shop's US customer base suggests most TikTok Shop buyers already shop on Amazon too. That matters for strategy — you're rarely choosing between two separate customer pools, you're choosing where in the funnel you want to meet the same shopper.

3. Which is cheaper to sell on?

TikTok Shop's headline commission is usually lower than Amazon's referral fees, but the full cost picture is closer once you add creator commissions and content production.

Amazon's referral fees and FBA costs are largely fixed once you know your category, which makes the total cost predictable. TikTok Shop's platform commission is lower on paper, but affiliate commissions to creators, sampling, and content production are real, ongoing costs that don't show up in a simple fee schedule. Brands that treat TikTok Shop as "commission-only" tend to underestimate what it actually costs to keep content flowing.

Budget for both the commission and the content pipeline from day one: sample units, creator payouts, and the time to brief, review, and iterate on videos. Treated that way, TikTok Shop's real cost sits closer to a paid media channel with a creator layer than a pure marketplace fee.

4. Which converts better?

Amazon converts higher-intent traffic faster because the shopper has already decided to buy something in that category. TikTok Shop conversion depends heavily on the strength of the content and the offer in the moment a viewer sees it.

That's why a TikTok Shop strategy built only around organic posting underperforms: once a piece of creator content proves it converts, it's worth putting paid spend behind it with Spark Ads and GMV Max rather than hoping the same video keeps working organically.

5. Which builds your brand faster?

TikTok Shop has the edge for brand-building because purchases happen inside creator storytelling, not inside a marketplace listing that looks like every competitor's.

On Amazon, the marketplace itself is the brand shoppers remember first. On TikTok Shop, a well-matched creator and a consistent content angle let a brand build recognition and loyalty that an Amazon listing page structurally can't. That's one reason a vetted creator network and a tested content angle matter more on TikTok Shop than ad budget alone.

6. Should your brand run both?

Most brands that succeed on either channel eventually run both: TikTok Shop for discovery and top-of-funnel growth, Amazon for capturing and repeating the demand TikTok Shop creates.

If you're resource-constrained, sequence it instead of splitting focus evenly: get one channel's unit economics working, then add the second once you have a repeatable process (and the content library) to support it. If your product is visual, impulse-friendly, and you can commit to creator content, TikTok Shop is usually the faster channel to test first — see Setup & API Onboarding for how we get a US seller account compliant and live. For a fuller breakdown of what ongoing TikTok Shop management actually involves once you're running it, see our guide on TikTok Shop management services.

One more reason the sequencing matters: content made for TikTok Shop and listings made for Amazon generally shouldn't be the same asset. Amazon rewards polished, standardized product imagery; TikTok Shop rewards native-feeling, creator-shot video. Plan for two content tracks rather than trying to stretch one set of assets across both channels.

Frequently asked questions

Is TikTok Shop better than Amazon for brands?
Neither is universally better. Amazon wins on scale and purchase intent, while TikTok Shop wins on discovery and brand-building through creator content. Most US brands run both rather than choosing one.
Is TikTok Shop cheaper than Amazon?
TikTok Shop's headline commission is often lower than Amazon's referral fees, but once creator commissions and content production are added in, total channel cost can land close to or above what you'd pay on Amazon.
Does TikTok Shop convert as well as Amazon?
Amazon generally converts shoppers faster because they arrive already searching with intent. TikTok Shop relies on creator content to turn a scroll-stopping moment into a purchase, so conversion depends more on content quality and offer design.
Should a new brand start on TikTok Shop or Amazon?
If you have proven search demand and limited content capacity, start on Amazon. If your product is visual and impulse-friendly and you can commit to creator content, test TikTok Shop first or launch both together.
