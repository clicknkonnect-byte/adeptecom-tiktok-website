FAQ

Questions brands ask before they send it

01Is the audit really free? What’s the catch?
It’s free and there’s no obligation. The audit is how brands get to see how we think before deciding anything. You keep the fix list whether or not you ever work with us, and nobody will chase you with a hard sell.
02What exactly will I receive?
A written, prioritized fix list for your shop. It covers listings and compliance, your creator and affiliate program, Spark Ads and GMV Max, and LIVE. Each item says what’s wrong, why it matters, and what to do first. If it helps, we’re happy to walk you through it on a short call.
03How fast will I hear back?
Within one business day of your request. If you message us on WhatsApp, we can usually start sooner.
04Do I need to give you access to my TikTok Shop or ad account?
No. We start from what’s visible: your shop, listings, product pages, and content. Access only comes up if you later choose to work with us, and then it runs through TikTok’s official seller APIs and permissions, never shared passwords.
05I already have an agency. Can I still request one?
Yes, and many brands use the audit as a confidential second opinion. We don’t contact your current agency, and your details stay private.
06My shop isn’t live yet. Is this still useful?
Yes. For pre-launch brands we review your catalog, category and documentation requirements, and the creator angle, so you launch clean and avoid the rejections that slow most new shops down.
07Which categories do you work with?
Beauty and skincare, health and supplements, home, pet, fashion and jewelry, baby, and more. Some categories need extra approval on TikTok Shop. If your product isn’t eligible, we’ll tell you straight away instead of wasting your time.
08Can you guarantee results?
No honest agency can promise a GMV number before seeing your products. What we can show is what the work has delivered: $52M+ in TikTok Shop sales, $38M+ through our affiliate network, and single-month results like $1.5M for a health brand and $700K for a beauty brand.
